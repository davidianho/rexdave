Diagnosing Wave Electronics' 2020 Revenue Shortfall

Business problem

A sharp drop in sales and revenue, with no clear view of why

Wave Electronics recorded a significant fall in revenue in 2020. Business executives needed a simple, interactive dashboard that could compare 2020 performance against the previous year at a glance, identify the countries, products and time periods responsible for the decline, and surface potential revenue opportunities so leadership could make informed decisions quickly, without digging through raw data.

Executive questions

The dashboard was designed around five questions

  • How did the business perform in 2020 compared with the previous year?
  • Which countries contributed most to revenue, and where did performance decline?
  • When did revenue performance begin to deteriorate?
  • Which products generated the most revenue, and which experienced the largest decline?
  • How much potential revenue is being missed from products that generated no sales?

Revenue performance by country

Every market declined — some far more than others

The United States remained the largest market by revenue, but like every country in the portfolio, it was hit by the year-over-year decline. Italy fared worst, while France held up best relative to the rest of the business.

Monthly revenue trend analysis

The two years diverge sharply after April

Revenue tracked a similar, softly declining path to the previous year through the first quarter. From April onward, 2019 saw a sharp recovery and climbed for the rest of the year — but 2020 never followed that pattern, staying flat instead of rebounding.

Missed revenue opportunity

Thousands of product lines never sold a single unit

Nearly 4,600 SKUs recorded no sales anywhere in the store network for the full year — representing an estimated $1.47M in potential revenue based on the dashboard's opportunity calculation. This flags an assortment or visibility problem worth investigating alongside the broader revenue decline.

Top & declining products

Where revenue concentrated, and where it fell fastest

This surfaces the five products generating the most revenue alongside the five experiencing the sharpest year-over-year decline.

Key insights

What the numbers actually say about the decline

Revenue declined 49.11% YoY while profit fell 49.08% YoY. The two moved almost in lockstep, meaning profitability didn't deteriorate disproportionately relative to sales; the drop was driven by volume, not margin erosion.
America remains the largest market. Italy posted a 56.60% YoY revenue decline the steepest of any market while France's 31.54% decline was the smallest, making the decline fairly consistent across all markets.
4,592 product lines generated zero revenue in 2020, representing an estimated $1.47M in potential revenue pointing to need for deeper analysis.
