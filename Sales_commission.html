Payout Analysis

As the Sales Analyst, I have been tasked with analyzing our company's May 2025 sales performance to calculate commission earnings for each sales employee. The Sales Manager requires this analysis to verify payouts, compare departmental performance, and evaluate commission plan effectiveness.

Commission Calculation Requirements

Base Rate Only

Calculate 5% commission on total sales value

Base Salary + Commission

Calculate 2% commission on total sales value (base salary reported separately)

Draw Against Commission

Calculate 3% commission on total sales value, accounting for any draw deductions

Gross Margin Based

Calculate 6% commission on gross margin (requires cost analysis)

Tiered Commission Structure

Apply progressive rates based on individual transaction values:

Amount Range Percentage

£333,334 – £666,666 3%

£800,001 – £1,200,000 5%
£1,200,001 – £1,600,000 6%
£1,600,001 – £2,000,000 7%
£2,000,001 – £10,000,000 8%

Tertiary Volume Commission

Apply regional rates to total sales:

  • North America & Europe: 4%
  • Africa, Asia & Middle East: 3%
